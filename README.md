# The Zyn–Magnesium Protocol

> Lock in, then clock out.™

## Abstract

We describe a daily routine combining sunlight, prayer, nicotine pouches, and magnesium gummies. Self-reported outcomes include increased energy, reduced appetite, and improved sleep. Participants (n=2, the authors) also report the removal of "potential nanobots blocking firmware updates to the brain". Results were not independently verified. Nobody asked them to be.

## Methodology

1. Wake.
2. Sunlight.
3. Prayer.
4. Take a zyn.
5. Work in flow state.
6. Take 2 magnesium gummies a couple of hours before bed.
7. Repeat.

Full procedure: [method](./method)

## Results

Anecdotal, enthusiastic, and uncontrolled. See [results](./results).

## Limitations

- Sample size: two, both of them the authors.
- No control group. The control group was a long time ago.
- Flow state was self-assessed by the person in it.
- Nanobot claims are unfalsifiable, which is to say, convenient.
- Sources include one dissent, a Grok post. See [sources](./sources).
