## Abstract

We describe a daily routine combining sunlight, prayer, nicotine pouches, and magnesium gummies. Self-reported outcomes include increased energy, reduced appetite, and improved sleep. Participants (n=2, the authors) also report the removal of “potential nanobots blocking firmware updates to the brain.” Results were not independently verified. Nobody asked them to be.

## The order

Same routine as the notes, in checklist order. The notes are quoted above and were not rewritten.

What you need. Sunlight. Prayer, which the notes mark as optional. A nicotine pouch, brand unnamed, and not a recommendation. Two magnesium gummies. Somewhere to sleep. Some work to do.

When, not how much.

1. Wake.
2. Sunlight, outside.
3. Prayer, if that’s your thing. Skipping it changes no measured result, because nothing here was measured carefully.
4. The pouch, once you feel like it. The notes name a brand and then say it is not an endorsement.
5. Work. The notes call this flow. One real task counts. So does one tab.
6. Two gummies, a couple of hours before bed. The notes say this takes the edge off quickly. They do not say how quickly.
7. Sleep. The notes allow another prayer here. It is also optional.

“Wake up energized” is the next morning, and it is the result the notes hope for. It is not a box you can check tonight.

Stopping rule. If you feel bad, stop. Nothing here says to take more.

Out of order. Gummies checked, pouch not checked: the list flags it. The notes put the pouch in the day and the gummies before bed. Do not uncheck a true box to make the order look right.

## Discussion

The plain account: a stimulant during the day, and two gummies before bed. Appetite went down, which is what nicotine does. Sleep improved, which is what the gummies are for. Energy is the extra claim people add when those two have already happened.

The nanobot sentence stays. It cannot be tested. A reply is linked below, under Sources. It was read. The sentence was not removed.

Flow was judged by the person claiming it. There is no record of the authors from before they started. That missing stretch is the control group.
